## Design Context

### Users
Academic Jarvis is a private, single-user workspace for Darius during his final year at LAM. It is used for quick checks on a phone or tablet and for focused planning on a Windows laptop.

### Brand Personality
Calm, capable, and transparent. Jarvis should feel attentive without pretending certainty, and proactive without taking control away from the student.

### Aesthetic Direction
A quiet academic operations desk: crisp typography, paper-like light surfaces, dark ink navigation, and restrained blue, green, amber, and coral signals. It should feel more like a well-made study instrument than a sci-fi assistant or generic SaaS dashboard.

### Design Principles
- Put verified work, dates, and source health before decoration.
- Make uncertainty and approval boundaries visible at the point of action.
- Keep repeated workflows compact, scannable, and keyboard/touch friendly.
- Use full-width lists and clear hierarchy; reserve cards for genuinely discrete records.
- Preserve every core workflow on mobile with 44px touch targets and readable text.
